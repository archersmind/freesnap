隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日

FREESNAP 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

在你的手机上处理

录屏画面和导入的视频只在你的 iPhone 上用来拼接长图，不会离开设备。生成的长图保存在 App 内和你选择保存到的「照片」里。FREESNAP 没有账号系统，也不使用任何分析、广告或第三方统计工具。

权限

  • 添加到照片：只用于把长图存入相册，FREESNAP 不能读取你的照片。
  • 通知：只用于在本机提示「长图已就绪」，不涉及推送服务器。
  • 屏幕录制：由你在控制中心开始和结束，画面只在本机分析。

购买

FREESNAP PRO 由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理付款，FREESNAP 不会获得你的付款信息。

联系

如有疑问，请联系 ctgtech2036@outlook.com。本政策如有变更，会更新本页并修改生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

FREESNAP does not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

Processed on your iPhone

Screen recordings and imported videos are used only on your iPhone to stitch long images and never leave the device. Long images are kept in the app and in Photos when you save them there. FREESNAP has no accounts and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Add to Photos: used only to save long images; FREESNAP cannot read your photos.
  • Notifications: used only for the local "long image ready" alert; no push server is involved.
  • Screen recording: started and stopped by you from Control Center; frames are analysed on the device only.

Purchases

FREESNAP PRO is paid through Apple's App Store; FREESNAP never receives your payment details.

Contact

Questions: ctgtech2036@outlook.com. If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you start

Does FREESNAP scroll the page automatically?

No. You scroll at your own pace, and FREESNAP finds the overlapping areas in the recording and stitches them together.

Why did capture not stop after I stopped scrolling?

A playing video can keep the recording active. Tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen and choose Stop; the captured content can still become a long image.

Can I import an existing screen recording?

Yes. Import an existing recording into FREESNAP to stitch, crop, and export it.

Are my screenshots uploaded to a server?

No. Processing happens on-device, and FREESNAP does not upload your recordings or long images.
